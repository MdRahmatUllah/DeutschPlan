## Bangla, Russian and Polish markets (researched 2026-09-30; Play fetched with hl/gl set to BD/RU/PL, but not from those countries; no search-volume tool)

### Bangla speakers (Bangladesh, West Bengal)
- **Who's there:**
  - Goethe-Institut Bangladesh (Dhaka, A1–B2 courses, every exam; a third party puts the A1 exam at 14,200 BDT) — goethe.de/ins/bd; sagadeutsch.com/german-exam/bangladesh.
  - Schools: German Schule mit Goni (germanschulemitgoni.com: "Best German Language Course in Bangladesh", visa/Ausbildung/nurse, "10,000+ students" by its own claim); Shield Language Academy (dhakalanguage.com/germanbangla, the Bangla title «জার্মান ভাষা শিক্ষা কোর্স»); BGLI (bgli.org, A1–B1).
  - **Shopnil Academy, "German for Bangla Speakers"** (shopnilacademy.com/courses/german-bangla): free web course promising A1–C2, but only A1 is live. **The closest rival in positioning, web-only and unfinished.**
  - YouTube: many Bangla A1 playlists (one channel run by a former Goethe-Institut Dhaka language head); Udemy "German A1 course for beginners in Bangla"; Bangla press (Prothom Alo, NTV) says Ausbildung needs B1.
- **Play** ("learn german bangla", gl=BD):
  - Bangla-specific: German Learning From Bangla (DigBazar, 10K+, 3.9★, a 500-word phrasebook) and Learn German with Adian Sir (5K+, A1–B1).
  - Generic apps under Bangla-localized titles: FunEasyLearn as «জার্মান ভাষা শিখুন», 5M+.
- **The gap:** no app is a full A1–C2 course with Bangla meanings; nothing offers **Bangla-letter pronunciation + offline + mock exams**.
- **Local SEO is weak:** the Bangla sites carry `lang="en"`, no hreflang, and no .bd domains, so our `lang="bn"` + hreflang is already ahead.
- **Keywords seen:** জার্মান ভাষা শিক্ষা · জার্মান ভাষা শেখা · জার্মান ভাষা শেখার অ্যাপ · বাংলায় জার্মান ভাষা শিখুন · জার্মান ভাষা কোর্স ঢাকা · আউসবিল্ডুং জার্মান ভাষা B1 · "German A1 Bangla" · "Goethe A1 Bangladesh".

### Russian speakers
- **Who's there:**
  - Free sites: Lingust (lingust.ru: free web course since 2011, `ru-ru` + hreflang, no exam focus); start-deutsch.ru (free "real exams A1–B2", Goethe/telc/ÖSD).
  - Schools: Deutsch Online (deutschonline.ru, a school A1–C1 with Goethe/telc/TestDaF/DSH, big social reach); Berliner Deutsch (paid web course, A1 5,600 ₽); Skyeng (tutors from 1,090 ₽ per lesson).
  - Goethe-Institut's free offline VHS apps "A1-Deutsch" and "B1-Deutsch".
  - Visa-driven demand: «Start Deutsch A1 для визы», «воссоединение семьи».
- **Play** ("немецкий язык", gl=RU): «немецкий язык с нуля» (gonliapps, 5M+, 4.7★), Todaii A1–C1, German Galaxy (Russian-native, 5.0★ from 546, B1+ still in development), ReWord, «Учить немецкий - 11.000 слов».
- **The gap:** no offline A1–C2 course in Russian with mock exams; **C1–C2 self-study is thin**.
- **Keywords:** немецкий язык с нуля · учить немецкий язык приложение · немецкий язык офлайн / без интернета · немецкий язык A1–C2 · подготовка к экзамену Goethe B1 · Start Deutsch 1 подготовка · немецкий для воссоединения семьи · немецкий для переезда в Германию.

### Polish speakers
- **Who's there:**
  - Subscriptions: eTutor (etutor.pl: A1–C1, 700+ lessons; our fetch got 403); Babbel's Polish content pages (`lang=pl`, /aplikacja-do-nauki-jezyka-niemieckiego).
  - Free and official: nauka-niemieckiego.net (free blog-course A1–B2, "prosto, po ludzku i po polsku"); DeutschAkademie /pl (a free course plus paid A1–C2); Goethe-Institut Polska.
  - Paid courses and marketplaces: paid video courses (LangMasters), Preply/Superprof.
  - **A niche: carers (opiekunki) working in Germany**, served by agencies' free courses (Promedica24, pflegedeutsch.pl).
- **Play** ("niemiecki", gl=PL): «Niemiecki dla Początkujących» (gonliapps), Wlingua (4.9★), «Nauka Niemieckiego od Podstaw!» (mobiteach, 1M+, 35.4K reviews), Todaii, Lingzy A1–C2, Babbel, Memrise.
- **The gap:** eTutor stops at C1 (subscription); Babbel is subscription-based and not exam-led; most apps are beginner-only. No offline A1–C2 with Goethe-style mocks.
- **Keywords:** nauka niemieckiego · niemiecki od podstaw · niemiecki dla początkujących · aplikacja do nauki niemieckiego · nauka niemieckiego offline / bez internetu · egzamin Goethe B1 przygotowanie · niemiecki A1–C2 · niemiecki do pracy w Niemczech · niemiecki dla opiekunek.

### Opportunities (the researcher's, my editing)
1. **Own the Bangla niche on Play.** A bn-localized listing titled like «Sogda: জার্মান ভাষা শিক্ষা A1–C2 (অফলাইন)», with a short description around «বাংলায় জার্মান ভাষা শিখুন».
2. **Localize the ru/pl Play titles** with «с нуля до C2 / офлайн» and «od podstaw do C2 / offline». Localized titles top both stores.
3. **Bangla Ausbildung/B1 page:** «আউসবিল্ডুং-এর জন্য জার্মান B1» → the B1 mock exams.
4. **A "Goethe A1 Bangladesh" page in Bangla:** the exam's format and our A1 mocks as practice before the paid exam.
5. **Bangla-letter pronunciation as the hook:** «বাংলা অক্ষরে জার্মান উচ্চারণ». No competitor has it.
6. **Russian visa-intent pages:** Start Deutsch 1 / family reunion; Goethe B1 prep, offline.
7. **Claim C1–C2** in ru/pl, where local rivals stop at C1 or earlier.
8. **A Polish carers page:** «niemiecki dla opiekunek — offline, w telefonie» (the data-limit angle is an assumption).
9. **Lead with "offline / без интернета / bez internetu / অফলাইন"**: only the VHS A1/B1 apps and FunEasyLearn advertise it, and none is A1–C2.
10. **Trust in each market:** exam alignment, content counts (5,069 words, 182 topics beat "500 words" and "700 lessons"), no account; ratings once they exist. No "#1" claims.
11. **YouTube shorts in Bangla**, e.g. «A1 শব্দ + বাংলা উচ্চারণ», where Bangla learners already are.
