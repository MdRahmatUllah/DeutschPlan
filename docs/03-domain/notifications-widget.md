# Reminders, background work and the home-screen widget

## Reminder

`services/reminder_notifications.dart` (`flutter_local_notifications`) posts it; `data/repositories/reminder_scheduler.dart` keeps its schedule. One daily notification at `reminder_time` on study days. Today is a study day as it was planned: a change to the study days applies from tomorrow (BR-PLAN-08), so switching today off keeps today's reminder and switching it on over a rest day adds none (#751). With `reminder_only_when_due = 1` (default) the text is built from the real plan at send time by a `workmanager` task scheduled 10 minutes earlier: "12 revisions · 7 new · about 9 min" (+ "Grammar due: …"); if the day is already complete or nothing is due, the notification is cancelled. Tap → `sogda://today`. Permission is requested only when the learner switches the reminder on.

Details the reminder settles (#157):
- `ReminderScheduler` (`data/repositories/reminder_scheduler.dart`) keeps the schedule to `reminder_enabled`, `reminder_time`, `study_days_mask` and `ui_language`: synced when the app starts and again on each change, nothing scheduled while the reminder is off. Syncs run one after another: restart setup writes three of those settings at once, and an "on" still scheduling while the "off" cancels would leave reminders the learner turned off. A sync cancels the reminder's own notifications only, its scheduled ones and those shown on its channel, never the app's others: the model download's shares the app (#509).
- It schedules the next week's study days as one notification each, at the local `reminder_time` of each date (`domain/reminder_times.dart`), so a daylight-saving change can't shift one by an hour. Today's is included only while it is still ahead. A week is what's scheduled; `plan_pregenerate` (#158) keeps it rolling for a learner who doesn't open the app.
- `ReminderNotifications` (`services/reminder_notifications.dart`) is `flutter_local_notifications`: an inexact alarm on Android (`inexactAllowWhileIdle`, so no exact-alarm permission), a UNUserNotificationCenter request on iOS. A reminder the plugin refuses as past, today's a few milliseconds late, is left out, and the rest of the week still goes in (#693 SV-6). The scheduled reminders come back after a reboot (`ScheduledNotificationBootReceiver`, `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`).
- It never asks for the permission. `NotificationPermission` asks when the reminder is switched on (S2 page 5, and M5 in #147), and a phone that said no simply doesn't show the notification.
- Scheduled, the reminder reads "Time for German — Today's plan is ready. A few minutes is enough.", in the app language; `reminder_compose` writes the day's plan into it (below). `reminder_only_when_due` leaves the schedule as it is; cancelling a day with nothing due is that task's job.
- Each reminder's notification id is its date (`20260921`), so the task can replace or cancel today's alone.
- The small icon is `drawable/ic_notification.xml` (#602), the brand kit's mono tiles, white on transparent, since the status bar draws an icon from its alpha alone: `PlatformReminderNotifications.smallIcon`, the plugin's default, so the model downloads' closing notice (#506) shows it too. Only Dart names it, so `res/raw/keep.xml` keeps it through the release build's resource shrinking: stripped, the plugin's lookup finds nothing and the reminder fails to initialise. `background_downloader`'s own progress notification keeps its state icons (download, pause, done, error): its API takes no icon, only a same-named drawable in the app, and the tiles would say less than the state.
- A tap opens `sogda://today`, through `resolveDeepLink`: a tap while the app runs, and the one that starts it (the plugin's launch details).
- Each reminder is an instant, the local `reminder_time` when it was scheduled. A learner who changes time zone hears the old zone's 19:30 until the app next starts and reschedules.

## Background tasks (`workmanager`)

| Task | When | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `plan_pregenerate` | 00:05 local, daily | `openDay(today)` so the widget and reminder are accurate |
| `reminder_compose` | reminder_time − 10 min | builds/cancels the notification |
| `widget_refresh` | after every session, hourly for the word of the day while a widget is placed | writes the snapshot |

Details the background tasks settle (#158):
- `services/background_tasks.dart` runs them; `BackgroundWork` (`services/background_work.dart`) queues them. On Android each is WorkManager unique work named after the task. On iOS `plan_pregenerate` and `reminder_compose` are BGProcessingTasks and `widget_refresh` a BGAppRefreshTask, with the three ids in `BGTaskSchedulerPermittedIdentifiers` and registered in `AppDelegate`, and the `fetch` and `processing` background modes.
- `plan_pregenerate` and `reminder_compose` are one-offs that queue their own next run: the app queues tonight's 00:05 at every start, and `ReminderScheduler` queues the compose on every sync, ten minutes before the next reminder, or at once when the sync falls inside those ten minutes (it has just put the plain text back). A compose queues the one for the next study day's reminder after it. `widget_refresh` is periodic, hourly, and only while a widget is on the home screen (`home_widget`'s `getInstalledWidgets`; a query that fails counts as placed): with none, the app's start cancels it, and a run that finds the widget gone cancels itself (#711). A widget placed while the app is closed is written at 00:05 by `plan_pregenerate`, and hourly from the next start.
- The app queues them apart from the notifications plugin: one that fails to start costs the reminder, not the widget or the nightly chain (#625).
- `plan_pregenerate` runs `openDay(today)` and a reminder sync, so the week of reminders rolls on for a learner who doesn't open the app.
- `reminder_compose` reads T1's view of today. Its text is the open blocks and BR-PLAN-09's estimate, "12 revisions · 7 new · about 9 min", a block with nothing open left out, and "Grammar due: <first topic due>" on a second line (expanded on Android). Practice sentences don't count as due. When nothing of revise, new or grammar is open, which includes a finished day, it cancels today's reminder under `reminder_only_when_due`, and otherwise puts the plain one back, never a plan written in earlier that the day has since finished (#626). In the app, today's reminder follows the day: when a session finishes it, or an *Undo* opens it again, it is composed again at once (`followReminder`, #626). A rating that leaves the day open writes nothing, so a plan the compose wrote in keeps its counts until it rings (ponytail: no plugin call per card). On a rest day it cancels. When it runs after the reminder time, or with the reminder off, it writes nothing.
- A task runs in an engine of its own. It opens user.db on its own connection (not drift's shared isolate, which would end with the task's engine under an app that joined it meanwhile), with `busy_timeout` so its writes wait for the app's; the app's streams don't hear them, and only `openDay`'s rows are written. A task finds no course before the app's first start and does nothing. Nor does it migrate: a user.db not at this build's schema is left for the app's next start (#158, #641). So is a course this build hasn't installed yet: after an update, the course on disk stays the old build's until the app's next start installs the new one, and this build's SQL can fail on it (a column it lacks). A task that finds the bundled course isn't the installed one (`ContentUpdater.pending`, the check the start makes) skips (#1018). So after an update that moves the schema or ships a new course, which is nearly every release, a learner who doesn't open the app gets the reminders already scheduled, for up to a week, and then none until the next app start, which migrates the file, installs the course and rolls the week on again. A start that can't copy the new course (#617, a full disk) leaves the old one, and the tasks keep skipping until an install succeeds. That is accepted (the owner, 2026-09-28, #976): a learner who ignores a week of reminders has stopped, and the next start catches up. Either way a skipped `plan_pregenerate` still queues tomorrow's, so the chain outlives the skip (#625).
- iOS runs its tasks when it chooses, never before the time asked. A compose that runs late leaves the plain reminder.
- `widget_refresh` writes the widget's snapshot (below), and so does `plan_pregenerate` after `openDay`.

## Widget (`home_widget`)

The app writes `widget_snapshot.json` ({step, remaining, total, minutes, wordOfDay{uid, article, german, meaning}, done}) to the shared container; Glance (Android) and WidgetKit (iOS) render it. Sizes: small 2×2 (ring, "8 left", step), medium 4×2 (adds Wort des Tages with a pronounce action that deep-links to `sogda://word/<uid>?speak=1`). Word of the day = a learned word due soon, never a To-do word. When the day is done the widget shows the Lime check and tomorrow's preview.

Details the snapshot settles (#159):
- `services/widget_snapshot.dart` builds it from T1's view of today: `{date, step, remaining, total, minutes, done, wordOfDay{uid, article, german, meaning}, tomorrow{revise, newWords, minutes}, copy{…}}`. `remaining` and `total` are the ring's (FR-T1-02) and `minutes` its estimate. `tomorrow` is set once the day is done, for the Lime-check state, and is null before. `date` lets a widget tell yesterday's snapshot from today's.
- It is stored as the JSON string under `widget_snapshot` in `home_widget`'s shared store: SharedPreferences on Android, and the App Group `group.de.sogda.app`'s UserDefaults on iOS (release.md). #160 and #161 read it there; on Android the write is followed by `updateWidget`, which redraws the widget at once.
- Word of the day (FR-X1-03, `domain/word_of_day.dart`): the learned words (learning or done, never To-do or suspended) due within three days. One is picked by a generator seeded with the date, over the uids in order, so it holds all day while the candidates do. A word revised today can leave them and move the pick. None, and `wordOfDay` is null. The meaning follows the meaning languages (#1081): the first (English where the course has none), and the second after it, as "flat, apartment · ফ্ল্যাট".
- Written at midnight (`plan_pregenerate`), hourly (`widget_refresh`), and in the app whenever the snapshot changes (`followWidget`, from launch). A finished session always changes it, which is FR-X1-01's "after every session". An unchanged snapshot isn't written again: the store compares it with what it holds, so a background task's unchanged snapshot is neither written nor redrawn (#711). A store that can't be written costs one refresh, never the task. The meaning follows the meaning languages as M3 changes them. An app left open across midnight reads the date again before it writes, so it never puts yesterday's snapshot over the new day's.

Details the Android widget settles (#160):
- A Glance widget (`android/…/widget/SogdaWidget.kt`) behind `home_widget`'s Glance receiver, which hands it the snapshot. `SizeMode.Responsive` draws two layouts: small below 250 dp wide, medium from there on. It places at 2 × 2 and stretches to 4 × 2 or more, uncapped, because cells differ by launcher. The small layout pins the step and the name to the top, and the ring to the bottom, as the artboard does.
- Its words come in the snapshot as `copy`, already in the learner's UI language: `app`, `left` ("8 left"), `leftStep` ("8 left · A2.1"), `progress` ("12/20"), `minutes`, `wordOfDay` (German in every language), `pronounce`, `done`, and `tomorrow` ("Tomorrow · 12 revisions · 7 new", once the day is done). The native side can't read the ARB files, and a snapshot written in the old language is rewritten when the language changes. Before the app has written one, the widget says so. That string, `widget_empty`, the picker's `widget_description` and its preview's `widget_preview_line` (#1098) are the widget's only Android strings: `res/values` (English) with `values-bn`, `values-pl` and `values-ru` (#1078). Being Android resources, they follow the phone's language, not the app's: the app in Polish on an English phone shows the picker in English, while the placed widget speaks the snapshot's `copy`, in the app language (#1195).
- Its colours are the tokens, written out in `res/values/colors.xml` and `values-night/colors.xml`: light, and dark when the phone is dark. The glass theme draws as its opaque fallback (FR-X1-04). `test/features/widget_native_test.dart` stops the two copies drifting, and checks that the redraw names the receiver the manifest declares. Glance has no determinate ring, so the arc is a white bitmap tinted Lagoon from a resource; the track and the done state's Lime check are drawables. Every colour is a resource, so a switch between light and dark shows at once.
- Done: the Lime check and "Done for today", then tomorrow's line. The line matches T1's Tomorrow card: "Tomorrow · 12 revisions · 7 new · 1 grammar topic", or T1's "Rest day tomorrow" for a rest day. A day with nothing planned has no line. With no word of the day, the medium size shows the minutes (or, done, tomorrow) in its place. The artboard doesn't draw that case.
- A snapshot from another day, or one written before `copy` existed, draws as `widget_empty`. The widget redraws when the launcher asks as well as when the app does. A day finished at 22:00 would otherwise still say "Done for today" the next morning, before `plan_pregenerate` has run.
- Every text is read null-safe. Android's `org.json` gives the string "null" for a JSON null, so a word without an article would have read "null bitte".
- A rest day draws what the snapshot counts: optional revisions show as "8 left", and an empty day as "0 left". The snapshot carries no study-day flag, and one comes when the widget must say "Rest day".
- The card is a rounded drawable, because Glance's `cornerRadius` does nothing below Android 12 (minSdk is 26).
- Taps open `MainActivity` with the link as its data (FR-X1-02): the widget opens `sogda://today`, the word opens `sogda://word/<uid>`, and *Pronounce* opens the same link with `?speak=1`. `deep_links.dart` routes them.
- Tests: the snapshot's `copy` in English and Bangla, tomorrow's line, the colours, and the Android save's redraw (the `home_widget` channel). There are no Flutter goldens for a native widget, so it was checked on the emulator at both sizes, light and dark, before and after the day was done. The picker's preview is `widget_preview.xml`, the small size's shape (`previewLayout`, Android 12+, #1070); below Android 12 it is the app icon. RemoteViews inflates only [its allowed classes](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/appwidgets/layouts#supported-layouts), so a plain `<View>` spacer made the picker say "Couldn't add widget." (#1090): a test fails on any other tag in the widget's own layouts. Its line beside the ring is its own short string, `widget_preview_line` ("Today's plan", in each app language), ending in "…" if a launcher cuts it: the full description lost "of the day" at three lines on the S24 (#1098). The picker's caption under it keeps `widget_description`.
