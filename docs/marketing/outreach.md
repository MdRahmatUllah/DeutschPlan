*agent-5, 2026-10-03. Who hears about Sogda once, politely, with the right pitch: the "best apps to learn German" lists that people and AI answers read, a few community and news desks, and AlternativeTo. Every target was checked on 2026-10-03 ([`research/2026-10-03-outreach-targets.md`](research/2026-10-03-outreach-targets.md), each with its URL). The list started as sogda-website #76, where the pitches were drafted in five languages.*

**The owner sends; agents never do.** Outreach waits for the Play link and the owner's go (O7, sogda-website #56). After that:
- **one message per site**, with no follow-up;
- the facts are from `site-facts.json`, never a rating, a user count or "free";
- the mock exams are "not official Goethe or telc papers".

## The pitch

**sogda-website #76 holds the reviewed drafts** in en, de, pl, bn and ru. Before sending, make the same three changes in each language, so they match the first release:
1. **Counts:** fill the numbers from `site-facts.json` at the release's tag. In pl and ru, write them as «label: count» (#1379), since the count changed from the drafts' and the noun must agree.
2. **The documents line:** add each language's line from `messaging.md` (*Learning from your own documents*).
3. **The link:** each target's own referrer, `<play-link email/pitch>`, with its site as `utm_content` if the owner wants per-site counts.

The en pitch, as it goes:

> **Subject:** An offline German course, A1 to C2, for your list of apps to learn German
>
> Hello <name>,
>
> I make **Sogda**, a German course app for Android that may fit your list: **the whole course works offline, with no account; A1 to C2 in {totals.steps} steps, with {totals.words} words, {totals.grammar_topics} grammar topics and {totals.mock_exams_per_step} mock exams for every step, and meanings and a pronunciation guide in English, Bangla, Russian or Polish.**
>
> Two things set it apart from the apps you list:
> - it's a whole course with exam practice, all on the phone, where many offline apps are phrasebooks;
> - it serves learners from Bangla, Russian and Polish in their own script.
>
> It also reads the German you meet: share or photograph a letter, and it marks the new words by level.
>
> The mock exams test listening, writing and speaking, plus vocabulary and grammar. They're not official Goethe or telc papers.
>
> Google Play: <play-link email/pitch> · Website: https://www.sogda.de · Press facts: https://www.sogda.de/en/about
>
> Happy to answer anything.
